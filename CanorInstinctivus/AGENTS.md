## Local Invariants & Rules

- **Role:** CanorInstinctivus integrates the CanorLinguae language suite, the CanorDatabasis invariant store, and CarmenInstinctus physical models into concrete operational workflows. It implements strategy engines, state excitation algorithms, and Ore algebraic extensions.
- **Local Rules:** All operations use integer hypervectors (1024-bit packed words) with similarity scores in `[0, CANOR_ONE]`. Conformance checks must decode and verify invariance.
- **Naming Standards:** Strictly `canor_*`. Legacy prefixes (`fc_*`, `vi_*`) are forbidden in all new and migrated code.
- **Arithmetic:** Pure integer and interval arithmetic. Floating-point types (`float`, `double`) are prohibited.
- **Status Codes:** Return unified `canor_status` with explicit diagnostics for undefined cases.
